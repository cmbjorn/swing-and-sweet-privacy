Swing & Sweet: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Swing & Sweet does not collect your data. It does not send anything anywhere: the app uses no network and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is stored on your Mac, in the app's own folder:

  • your settings (the knobs, the chosen output);
  • for each track you play, a few numbers about its loudness and tone, and your choices for it (Track Volume, Limiter only), stored under Spotify's ID for the track, so the app can level it better the next time; the notes also hold the track's title and artist as Spotify reports them;
  • logs for troubleshooting: numbers (loudness, performance) and the title and artist of the tracks played.

What it does not store

It never stores or records audio. The only audio it holds is a few seconds in memory, to process it, and it is gone when the app stops.

Other apps

To process Spotify's sound the app uses macOS's System Audio Recording permission, and to follow pause, skip and seek it asks Spotify what is playing (the Automation permission). Both stay on your Mac.

Deleting your data

Setup → Delete all notes removes the track notes. Everything else (the per-track numbers and choices, and the logs) is removed by deleting the app's folder: quit the app, then delete the Swing & Sweet folder in ~/Library/Containers/ (Setup → Show measurement log in Finder shows where it is).

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
