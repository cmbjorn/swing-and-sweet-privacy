Swing & Sweet: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Swing & Sweet does not collect your data. It does not send anything anywhere: the app makes no network connections of its own and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are bought through Apple's App Store, which handles the payment. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID has them; we receive no personal or payment data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is stored on your Mac, in the app's own folder:

  • your settings (the knobs, the chosen output);
  • for each track you play, a few numbers about its loudness and tone, and your choices for it (Track Volume, Limiter only), stored under the player's ID for the track (Spotify's or Music's), so the app can level it better the next time; the notes also hold the track's title and artist as the player reports them;
  • with the built-in player: the music folders you choose and, for each file in them, where it is, its tags, its tempo and a few numbers about its sound; your playlists and what is up next;
  • with the Restore Workshop: its list of recordings and, for each one it restored, a report of numbers;
  • logs for troubleshooting: numbers (loudness, performance) and the title and artist of the tracks played.

What it does not store

It never records the sound of other apps. The only audio it holds from them is a few seconds in memory, to process it, and it is gone when the app stops. The Restore Workshop writes restored copies of your own files, the ones you give it, into the folder you choose, and nowhere else. Its trained denoise model is part of the app and runs on your Mac (the Neural Engine); no sound is sent anywhere to be processed.

Other apps

To process the sound of the app you choose (Spotify, Music, or a browser or another app) the app uses macOS's System Audio Recording permission, and to follow pause, skip and seek in Spotify or Music it asks that player what is playing (the Automation permission). Both stay on your Mac. While it runs the app also moves the Mac's alert sounds to the built-in speakers, so notifications do not reach the sound system, and moves them back when it stops.

Deleting your data

Setup → Delete all notes removes the track notes. Everything else (the per-track numbers and choices, and the logs) is removed by deleting the app's folder: quit the app, then delete the Swing & Sweet folder in ~/Library/Containers/ (Setup → Show measurement log in Finder shows where it is).

The website's waitlist

If you join the waitlist on the Swing & Sweet website, your email address (and, if you choose one, whether you are a DJ, a teacher or a listener) is sent to Christian by email through the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). It is used only to tell you when Swing & Sweet is out, and is not shared or sold. Write to the address below and it is deleted. The app itself never sees it.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
